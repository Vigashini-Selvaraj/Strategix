QUESTIONS

Before You Book.

Is this service suitable for beginners?

Yes, all our services can be tailored to match your current skill level, starting from strong fundamentals for beginners.

How long is each session?

Standard sessions are 60 minutes long, carefully structured to balance theory, practice, and review.

Can I choose online or in-person coaching?

Yes, depending on your location, you can opt for in-person sessions at our academy or high-quality online sessions.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes, sessions can be rescheduled with prior notice as per our rescheduling policy outline.

Can I cancel my plan?

You can cancel future renewals of monthly plans at any time through your dashboard.

What happens if I miss a session?

Missed sessions without prior notice are generally forfeited, but exceptions may apply for emergencies.

Can I upgrade my plan?

Yes, you can upgrade from a single session or monthly plan at any point by contacting our team.

When will my session be confirmed after payment?

Session confirmations are typically sent immediately via email and appear on your dashboard.

Can I request a specific coach?

Yes, subject to their availability. You can mention your preference during the booking process.

What payment methods are available?

We accept all major credit cards, UPI, and standard digital payment gateways through our secure checkout.
