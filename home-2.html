THE TRAINING METHOD

Every Session Has A Purpose.

Coaching isn't about filling an hour with moves. Every session is a carefully structured story designed around a specific improvement goal.

01

The Assessment

We begin by analyzing recent games to understand your current strengths, weaknesses, and psychological tendencies. We don't just look at the blundersâ€”we look at the thought process that led to them.

02

The Lesson

Once the flaw is identified, we introduce the strategic concept needed to fix it. This involves studying master games, understanding pawn structures, and learning the theory behind the correct approach.

03

The Application

Theory is useless without practice. We place you in carefully curated, complex positions against the coach. You must apply the newly learned concept in real-time under simulated tournament pressure.

04

The Review

Finally, we analyze your decisions from the practical session. Did you apply the principle correctly? Were you distracted by tactical traps? We refine your understanding until the concept becomes second nature.
