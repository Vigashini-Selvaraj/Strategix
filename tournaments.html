QUESTIONS

Before You Get Started.

What service is right for a beginner?

For beginners, we typically recommend Private Coaching or our introductory Group Coaching sessions. This allows us to establish strong foundational principles before introducing advanced theory.

Can I combine coaching with tournament preparation?

Absolutely. Many of our students transition their standard coaching sessions into dedicated tournament preparation modules in the weeks leading up to an event.

Do you offer online coaching?

Yes, our Online Coaching service delivers the exact same rigorous, structured curriculum as our in-person sessions via premium digital chessboard platforms and video calls.

Do you provide group or school programs?

Yes. We partner with educational institutions and organizations to deliver structured, semester-long chess programs designed to build intellectual resilience and strategic thinking in students.

Can services be customized?

Every player is unique. We tailor the syllabus, pacing, and focal points of our private coaching entirely around your personal objectives and current skill level.

How do I book a session?

Simply navigate to our Contact page or click "Book A Free Trial". We'll arrange a quick consultation to understand your needs and match you with the right program.
