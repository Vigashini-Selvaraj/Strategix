Before You Contact Us.

How do I choose the right program?

If you're unsure which program fits your current skill level and goals, we highly recommend booking a free trial. During the trial, our coaches will assess your play and suggest the most effective path forward.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. We offer an introductory session where you can experience our coaching style, meet the team, and receive a preliminary assessment.

Can I ask about pricing before booking?

Absolutely. You can select "Pricing" in our enquiry form above to receive our complete fee structure, or explore our Programs page for base pricing details.

Do you provide online coaching?

Yes. Our online coaching programs provide the exact same premium curriculum and structured methodology as our in-person sessions, conducted via professional video conferencing and digital boards.

Do you offer school or organization programs?

We partner with select educational institutions and corporate organizations to deliver specialized strategy workshops and chess curriculums. Please use the form above to discuss requirements.

How can I register for a tournament?

Upcoming tournaments are listed on our Tournaments page where you can register directly. For large groups, please contact us for streamlined registration.

How long does it take to receive a response?

Our team reviews enquiries daily. You can expect a professional response within 24 business hours.

Can I request a specific coaching schedule?

Yes, private coaching offers flexible scheduling. Group sessions are held at fixed times, but we maintain multiple batches to accommodate different time zones and availability.
